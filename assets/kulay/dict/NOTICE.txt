Kulay word meanings
===================
The files in this folder are built from Open English WordNet 2025
(https://en-word.net), which is released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Open English WordNet is derived from Princeton WordNet. Princeton WordNet is
copyright 2006 by Princeton University and is used under the WordNet licence
(https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use).

Changes made for Kulay: only single lowercase words are kept; each word has at most
2 meanings per part of speech (5 in all); long definitions are shortened; meanings about
sex or containing slurs are removed; one simple synonym is chosen per meaning. The
build script is tool/build_dictionary.py.
